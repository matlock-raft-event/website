# The Matlock Raft Event design language

The rules behind the Matlock Raft Event site: what it's allowed to look like, and why.
If a choice isn't covered here, the principles decide. The logo is fixed and everything
else exists to frame it.

## Principles

1. **Village fête, not startup** — every element should feel at home on a riverside
   noticeboard on Boxing Day. If it would look right in a SaaS pitch deck, it's wrong here.
2. **The river runs through it** — water is the connective tissue: wave dividers, the river
   surface, the panorama. Sections don't just stack, they flow downstream.
3. **The logo is the mascot** — the badge supplies the whimsy. Typography, colour and layout
   stay confident and simple so the badge (and the photos) can be the characters.
4. **Say it once, clearly** — each fact lives in exactly one place per screen. Copy is
   truthful, warm and plain-spoken; invented claims are not on-brand.
5. **Things are physical** — buttons press, cards tilt like pinned photos, the badge hangs.
   Depth comes from hard offset shadows, never glows.
6. **Loud in one place** — one big moment per screen. Everything around it stays quiet so
   the loud thing lands.

## Typography

Two faces, no exceptions:

- **Anton** (display) — headlines, big numbers, card titles. Single weight; never pair with bold utilities.
- **Albert Sans** — everything else. Body is regular/medium sentence case at a 62–74ch
  measure; labels are ExtraBold caps with +0.12 to +0.2em tracking. That single label
  treatment covers buttons, nav links, eyebrows, chips, table headers and form labels.

## Motion

Interactions 130–250ms ease; reveals (the `Reveal` component) are a single 700ms
rise-and-fade on first view. Wave seams are **static by choice** — ambient motion is
limited to the marquee. Everything respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Voice

Warm, concrete, a bit daft, always true. Labels in caps, sentences in sentence case,
one exclamation mark per page maximum.
